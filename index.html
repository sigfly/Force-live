How it works

Force = mass × acceleration. The sensor measures acceleration in m/s², which is multiplied by the mass above to produce newtons. The default 1 kg is a reference mass, not the weight of your phone. Motion acceleration excludes gravity when supported; otherwise readings include gravity and are marked as such. These are estimates of inertial force, not a direct force measurement.
